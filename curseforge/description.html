Tavern Dice: ten thousand at the tavern table

Pull up a chair and play 10,000, the old tavern dice game also known as Farkle, Dix Mille, Zilch or Greed. Roll six dice, set aside the ones that score, then push your luck or bank your points. Roll nothing that scores and it's a Farkle: the turn's points are gone. Made for WoW Forever and roleplayers.

Features

  • Play a friend, near or far: invite anyone with the add-on (right-click their name, target them, or one click for your Tjommie buddy). Works across realms and factions with Battle.net friends.
  • Find a game: see who nearby (and in your group or guild) has Tavern Dice, and invite them with one click. Their tooltip tells you too.
  • Tavern regulars: play solo against up to three bots: Old Barnaby (cautious), Mira Ashvale (balanced) and Grizzle Sparkfuse (reckless).
  • House rules: Classic, or Mumpits (bank 400+ every turn; two 5s make 100 and one goes back in the cup). Play to 2,000, 5,000 or 10,000, with an optional minimum for your first bank.
  • Fair dice: optionally every throw is a real server /roll, read as six dice in one roll. In a group, each player's add-on checks the other's rolls.
  • Table talk: when your friend is beside you, your character shouts "Hot dice!", cheers when you win and sighs or grunts at a Farkle. Quiet when you're far apart.
  • The table: wooden table, green felt, tumbling dice, a ledger, a tavern log that narrates every throw, Farkle and Hot Dice pop-ups.
  • Your record at the table, minimap button, and a bug log you can copy (/dice bugs).

Score with all six dice for hot dice: scoop them all up and keep rolling. When someone passes the target, everyone else gets one last turn.

Commands

  • /dice: open the table
  • /dice invite Name: invite a friend (or your target)
  • /dice tjommie: invite your tjommie
  • /dice leave: leave a friend game
  • /dice findme: let nearby players find you (on/off)
  • /dice stats: your record
  • /dice bugs: copy the bug log

Both players need Tavern Dice installed to play together. Gold bets are up to you: the add-on keeps score, it never moves gold.
